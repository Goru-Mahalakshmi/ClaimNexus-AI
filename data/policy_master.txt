Clause 1.1 (Accidental Damage Coverage): The policy covers accidental damage from external collisions, provided the fundamental facts and impact vectors of how the collision occurred are consistent across all evidentiary records and police documentation.
Clause 2.1 (Required Submission Documents): Mandatory submission documents include the official Claim Form, Customer Incident Description, and a certified Repair Estimate from an authorized garage.
Clause 2.2 (FIR Requirement): An official First Information Report (FIR) or police report must be provided when third-party involvement, hit-and-run, or moving vehicle collisions are claimed. Its contents must align precisely with the customer's primary accident narrative.
Clause 3.1 (Claim Submission Window): All claims must be submitted in writing within 30 days of the incident date. Late filings without justified emergency cause are subject to rejection.
Clause 4.1 (Insured Declared Value - IDV): The IDV represents the maximum liability of the insurer. Repair costs exceeding 75% of IDV trigger a constructive total loss evaluation.
Clause 5.1 (Deductibles and Excess): A standard deductible of $500 applies to all collision claims unless waived by specific policy add-on riders.
